Companion to docs/commissioner-linebreak-measurement.md. Every number there is settled; these four things are not, because they are eye calls. Both faces are embedded — this file works offline.

01The hero sentence, and where its width band went

Three sentences lose their single-line window. The middle column is the control: Bricolage with width neutralised. It is nearly identical to Commissioner, which is the whole finding — the break is the missing wdth axis, not the new typeface. The question for you is whether the two-line setting on the right is a loss worth trimming copy to avoid, given that every one of these is already two lines above 1000px today.

02The signature

The one place the 340/720 pair actually renders on the shipped site: the home hero at 60px. Note that the heavy half — consumer, enterprise, defense — contains no g, so the clumsy heavy-weight counter §7 worries about does not appear here. Measured ink says Commissioner 340/720 is 13% more weight contrast than Bricolage's, not less; 360 is the Commissioner weight whose ink matches Bricolage 340 almost exactly.

03The reading voice

Applying flare to body copy costs 3 of 1,209 measured line-sets — 0.2%. On re-fitting grounds the answer is free either way, so this is purely whether rungs 0–2 should look like the same family as rungs 5–6.

04Body size — 18 against 19

19px is derived from x-height matching in §6, and independently it is the measure-preserving size: Commissioner sets 4.3% narrower at 18px, and 19px cancels it, dropping new widows from 21 to 5. Two arguments, one number. Whether it reads as "the same size" or as "bigger" is the eye's call.
