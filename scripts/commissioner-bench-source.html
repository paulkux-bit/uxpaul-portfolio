Whether prose keeps family character, or reads as a second typeface

Fifty-six jurisdictions filed the same report and none of them agreed on what a boating accident was. The data was there. The system couldn’t speak it. Reading a paragraph at length is the only test that matters for a body face, because the eye forgives a specimen and does not forgive a page. Move the reading voice off zero and watch whether this warms toward the display rungs or starts to look noisy at 18px.

Zero is the safe default. The argument for ten or twenty is that flare is still perceptible in body text, and that a small amount keeps rungs 0–2 in the same family as rungs 5–6 rather than looking like a different typeface wearing the same name.

What this page cannot tell you

It renders one case study’s copy, so it cannot say what five shipped studies look like when two of them are still unwritten.
