We were losing trust where we charged the most.

In order to create a narrative for leadership to buy in, I dug into various data threads.

Standard shipping was dependable. The premium shipping method bought a worse chance of arriving on time. Orders out of stores were the worst of all.

I asked store operations what happens during a shift. Why weren't associates getting orders out on time? They already had a report, and it answered why a large chunk of orders were missing their dates: chaotic shifts, shipping SLAs nobody could hit, and not enough people rostered to send online orders out.

“I have to be extremely desperate to pay for expedited.”

Urban Outfitters customer, primary research

Miss the date and you lose trust. Customers remember the failure, and the longer we waited the harder it would be to change.

“I have really reduced my shopping on this site because of this.”

Anthropologie customer

I turned the numbers into a journey, with the moments customers got angry marked on it. That was the version leadership could see. My role through it was building the narrative and presenting it to executives.
