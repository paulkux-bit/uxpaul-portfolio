**Repo mirror.** The full record, including material deliberately kept out of

project knowledge base. This mirror exists because **the repo is what ships**,

## The source

**Deck p12** — "Our business performance / Out of stock error / Inventory update
delays causing out of stock scenarios during checkout." First of two speech
bubbles, upper right, attributed on the slide as **`-AN Customer`**.

Transcribed from the PDF at 8x magnification, not from memory. The sentence the
study uses is bolded here; the rest is context so the elision can be checked:

> "I love Anthro. BUT I'm so tired of placing things in my cart and then finding
> out the item is no longer available. And then it's STILL on the website a day
> later! **And, even worse, ordering, paying, and then 3-4 days later getting the
> email that the item wasn't actually available anymore.**
>
> Every time it happens I swear I'll never shop here again...one day I'll follow
> through! "

The shipped quote is that sentence, complete and verbatim. No elision, no
ellipsis, no case change.

## What was wrong before 27 Aug, and must not come back

An earlier version shipped as *"Ordering, paying, and then 3-4 days later getting
the email that the item wasn't actually available anymore."* — cited
*"Anthropologie customer, review."* Three defects:

**A silent elision.** *"And, even worse,"* was cut from the front of the sentence
with no marker. Restoring the three words is why the quote now needs no ellipsis
at all.

**A silent capitalisation.** `ordering` had been raised to `Ordering` so the
fragment would stand as a sentence.

**A citation claiming a channel the artifact does not support.** p12 carries two
kinds of evidence and separates them consistently:

| form | attribution on the slide | carries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| speech bubbles (x2) | `-AN Customer` | no username, no date, no star rating |
| boxed items (x2) | username + date + ★ rating | App Store reviews |

This quote is a **bubble**, so its channel is unknown — it could be a survey
verbatim, a customer-service capture, or an email. `AN` is the deck's own
abbreviation for Anthropologie, used as a column header on p11's missed-dates
table, so **"Anthropologie customer" is supported and "review" is not.**

---

## The straight apostrophe is the artifact's. Do not normalise it.

`wasn't` uses **U+0027**. The bubble's own typography is straight throughout —
`I'm`, `it's`, `wasn't` — verified at 8x. The two App Store reviews on the same
slide use **curly** apostrophes. Same slide, two typographies, because they are
two separately captured artifacts.

A quotation is transcribed per artifact and never normalised to a house
convention. The file's 48-straight / 1-curly census is a fact about the file, not
a rule that applies inside quotation marks.

The surrounding U+201C / U+201D double quotes are correct and stay.

## Deliberately not mirrored here

p12 carries three other customer verbatims: a second speech bubble, and two App
Store reviews about stock availability that no part of the study uses.

They are transcribed in the project doc and **not here, on purpose.** This
repository is public. The site does not publish reviewer usernames even for the
one App Store review it does quote, and the repo should not be looser than the
site. Unused internal deck verbatims have no reason to be published at all.

If a future pass needs one of them, read the project doc. Do not reconstruct one
from memory — that is the failure this whole file exists to prevent.
