<p className="section-lede">Nuuly members rent six garments a month, then send them back for six more.</p>

## I designed bright mode. They stayed on dim.

The floor wasn't lit the same everywhere, and people had their own preferences, so I designed a dim mode and a bright mode. I worked with the Android developers to see what the monitors could actually do. I was proud of bright mode.

Hardly anyone used it. They stayed on dim.

I still don't know why. Maybe it's what they were trained on. Maybe bright wasn't bright enough. Maybe dim just looked better in that room, and it stuck.
