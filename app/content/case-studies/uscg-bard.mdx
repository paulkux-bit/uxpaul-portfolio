## Investigators need to get every case right, but the system made that harder, not easier.

**Boating accident cases are rising**, and each one becomes a federal record, filed against a statutory deadline.

**This isn't just paperwork.** Any case could end up as evidence.

**The old system made required data easy to skip**, so cases went out breaking federal compliance rules.

<section className="cs-section">

## How do you unify fifty-six jurisdictions without changing one?

<p className="section-lede">Standardize the data, not the people.</p>

Forcing fifty-six jurisdictions onto a single form was an operational dead end. I kept local processes as they were and built a standard data structure underneath. Suddenly, trends and comparisons were possible.

</section>

<section className="cs-section">

## The form bends. The record underneath does not.

<p className="section-lede">Federal fields are required. Everything else is up to the jurisdiction.</p>

I designed a modular form on a single, shared data structure. Role-based views keep the interface clean. Local investigators see only what matters for their case, while Coast Guard reviewers get a high-level view with real-time compliance scoring.
