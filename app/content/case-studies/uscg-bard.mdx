## Every case is a federal record, and some end up in court.

More people are on the water than ever, so boating accidents are rising. A state investigator like Maria files every accident in her state as a case, against a legal deadline, and the record is hers.

**But the old system made it easy to skip required data,** so her cases came back from the Coast Guard for corrections she could have caught at entry.

## The old system failed Maria and the Coast Guard in three ways.

<p className="section-lede">I talked to twenty people who do the work, from the busiest states to the smallest territories, and read the old system's survey results and support tickets.</p>

<FrictionMoment work="degas-millinery-shop" />

Every attempt to align the fifty-six forms had died in planning, so nobody could compare one state's records with another's.

The Coast Guard compiled national figures by hand, worked from six-month-old numbers, and aimed safety campaigns at last season's problem. For Maria, filing one case meant cross-checking the record across four browser tabs, so the judgment part of her job waited.

<section className="cs-section cs-section--turn">

## How do you compare fifty-six jurisdictions without changing how any of them work?

<p className="section-lede turn-answer">Standardize the data, <span className="turn-answer__key">not the people.</span></p>

{/* ANTITHESIS BUDGET: BARD keeps one "X, not Y" line, this answer, which is the house

They were never all going to agree on one form. So I left local processes alone and designed a standard data structure underneath. A boating accident in Hawaii and one in Florida can finally be counted the same way.

</section>

<section className="cs-section">

## I designed a modular form, so each state files its own way into one record.

<p className="section-lede">Federal fields are required. Everything else is up to the jurisdiction.</p>

Maria and the Coast Guard read the same record for different reasons, and the form serves both. Maria can check her case the way the Coast Guard will read it. A compliance score fills in as she types, so she catches what used to come back for corrections. She sees only the fields her case needs.
