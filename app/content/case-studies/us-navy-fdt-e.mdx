<section className="cs-section">

## How do you trust what you can't question?

<p className="section-lede">The product team wanted a smarter model. I pushed for one analysts could check.</p>

**Doctrine binds the system, not the analyst.** I brought the research back and made the case for a verification gate. The team built it into the business rules. Nothing the model finds reaches an analyst until it clears the gate. Every step in it is one an analyst already answers to. Using the tool and following doctrine are the same thing.

</section>
