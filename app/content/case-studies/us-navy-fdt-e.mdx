## How do you trust what you can't question?

The product team wanted a smarter model. I used user research to keep us focused on what analysts would actually trust. By the end of discovery, it was clear: analysts won't act on an answer they can't check.

So we built military doctrine directly into the app's business rules. **Doctrine guides the system, not the analysts.** The model presents checklists and options, but the person always makes the call. They can escalate, get a second opinion, or trust their own read. FDT-E never delivers a verdict; it offers doctrine-backed choices, and the analyst decides what happens next.
