## Say one thing loudly. Say everything else quietly.

Because the person reading any surface is on their fortieth case of the week, not their first. Evaluated against Nielsen's heuristics. Held to Section 508 and WCAG 2.2 AA, computed mathematically across every pairing.

### The Insight Engine

The legacy dashboard was tables. Nothing surfaced a signal.

I built a system that reads the normalized data and writes one plain-language insight per surface. The same engine that writes one terse line on the dashboard writes a headline on the annual report.

### The form

One record, two readings. *Your View* for filing. *USCG View* for reviewing.

The toggle is the all-56-states rule made interactive: if any state populates a field in real submissions, the field stays. Verified against Illinois, Utah, and California.

### The system

Three-layer tokens for a twenty-five-year horizon. Status and sentiment kept parallel, not nested, so either can diverge without a refactor. Eight workflow states, each with their own hue, so color alone tells a reviewer who holds the case.

## What I got wrong.

I assumed a lean form was a better form. On the People form alone, eighteen fields looked dead by every measure I had. I checked them against real state submissions — Illinois, Utah, California. The "dead" fields were populated.

<blockquote className="pull-quote pull-quote--quiet">
  <p>A wireframe shows render state, not field inventory.</p>
</blockquote>

That reversal became the governing rule: one master form, and if any single state uses a field, it stays.

BARD doesn't save lives directly. It is the measurement and policy-input layer, not an intervention. Stronger data makes stronger safety policy possible — the causal chain runs through NASBLA and federal rulemaking, not from my interface to the water.

<PopUp anchor="reflection">
I tuned the entire Insight Engine voice to one persona. The analyst and executive surfaces don't exist yet. I don't know whether the voice I built belongs to the user or to the system. I'll find out when those surfaces ship.
</PopUp>
